01 — How it works

Three steps. At home. No needles.

The Eternal Flight cuff handles sampling and marking automatically. You never touch a needle.

Step 1

Sample

Wrap the cuff around your upper arm. It numbs the area and collects a rice-grain-sized sample of skeletal muscle in about ten seconds.

Step 2

Mark

The cuff applies a small memorial tattoo beside the sampling site. Choose a feather, a pair of wings, or your donor number.

Step 3

Return

Detach the sealed specimen cartridge, place it in the return capsule and drop it in any mailbox. It reaches our lab within 48 hours.

Inside the cuff

02 — From you to the sky

Seventy days from your arm to a wing.

Every donor's cells are grown in a dedicated line and never mixed with anyone else's.

Day 0–2Biopsy

Your sealed cartridge travels at 4 °C to the Eternal Flight cultivation lab.

  • Sample: ~0.5 g skeletal muscle
  • Transit: under 48 h, temperature logged
Day 2–5Isolation

Muscle stem cells (satellite cells) are isolated from the sample and screened.

  • Bacteria, mycoplasma and viral screening
  • Donor line opened under your ID
Day 5–40Expansion

Cells multiply in serum-free medium. Human cells can divide only a limited number of times, so every donor line has a natural ceiling.

Typical yield per donor: about 160 kg of tissue, fed across many batches.

Day 40–55Scale-up

Your line moves into its own bioreactor. Tags follow the vessel through every transfer.

Day 55–65Differentiation

Cells fuse into muscle fibres on an edible scaffold and mature into dense tissue.

Day 65–70Formulation

Tissue is fortified for vulture nutrition and checked for drug residues before release.

  • Fat and calcium added (wild vultures get calcium from bone)
  • NSAID residue test: must be undetectable
  • Vacuum-packed, labelled with donor ID and batch
Day 70+Feeding

Packs are delivered cold to your assigned feeding station and served on winter feeding days alongside other food.

You receive a notice with the date, station and batch.

05 — Pre-donation screening

Your body is part of their diet.

Some medicines that are safe for you can kill vultures. Please answer honestly.

Defer 14 days

Painkillers

Have you taken anti-inflammatory painkillers such as diclofenac or ibuprofen? Residues of these drugs in carcasses caused mass vulture deaths in South Asia.

Defer 30 days

Antibiotics

Recent antibiotic courses may affect cultivation and the birds' gut health.

Review

Lead exposure

Do you shoot, reload ammunition or work with lead? Lead poisoning is a major threat to scavenging raptors.

08 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Does it hurt?

The site is numbed before sampling. Most donors describe a brief pressure, then the hum of the tattoo module.

Is it still me after cultivation?

Your original sample is about 0.5 g. After expansion, less than 0.001% of what the vultures eat is tissue that was once inside your body. Every cell, however, carries your DNA and your donor ID.

Will the vulture know my name?

No. Your name is kept on the station wall and in your notices. The vulture only knows the meal.

Can I choose which bird eats my tissue?

You choose the station. Individual birds cannot be assigned; they feed in groups and in their own order.

Can I donate more than once?

Yes, after a 12-month interval. Each donation opens a new line and adds a new mark.
